# Google Play — Target Audience & Age Audit (TajikShop)

## What the app actually is (evidence)
General‑audience **e‑commerce / marketplace** for Tajikistan: product browsing, categories, cart, orders, seller storefronts, wallet/cashback, buyer–seller chat, reviews, cargo (China→TJ/RU) delivery, ads. No child‑oriented content, characters, or education‑for‑kids features. Contains user‑generated content and ads.

## Recommended Play Console answers (Target audience and content)
- **Target age group:** **18 and over** (recommended) — or 16–17 **and** 18+ at minimum. Rationale: the app involves purchases/payments, a wallet, buyer–seller chat, and third‑party ads; it is a commercial marketplace, not designed for children.
- **Is your app designed for children / appeal to children?** **No.**
- Do **not** opt into the **Designed for Families** program.
- Store listing must not use child‑directed imagery or claims.

## Consistency checklist (already true in code/docs)
- Privacy Policy has a **Children's privacy** section stating the app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data (`/privacy` §13).
- Ads (Yandex) are compatible with an adult/general audience.
- No age‑gate UI is required for an 18+ general marketplace, but you may add one if you later restrict content.

## Notes
- If you select an age band that **includes under‑18**, Google may apply additional policies (ads, Data Safety for minors). Selecting **18+** keeps the ads + marketplace + chat model straightforward and truthful.
- Do **not** falsely claim 18+ to avoid policy if the app is truly general — but for a payments/marketplace/chat app, 18+ (or "adults") is the honest, defensible choice.

**EXTERNAL ACTION REQUIRED:** set the Target Audience answers in Play Console per above.
